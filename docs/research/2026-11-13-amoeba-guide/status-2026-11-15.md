## 3. What we learned

1. **Carving is real at 27B scale.**
   - Learned cores beat random cores of the same shape by about 5× on English cost.
   - Boxes as small as 685 neurons restore a skill completely.
   - Every neuron can be accounted for without loss: with every gate open, the parts reproduce the full model exactly.
2. **A carved model can be faster than the original,** once tiles are fused and composed with speculation. The win is a cheaper verify step, which more than pays for the slightly lower acceptance.
3. **The carving corpus decides what lands in the core.** Repo text kept code in the core by construction. Graded prose puts technical text at the expensive edge, where it can become a box.
4. **Coordinates transfer across models, knowledge has not yet.**
   - Warp plus ridge maps translate hidden states between families: Qwen, Gemma and Flash-Next.
   - But injecting a mapped donor box into a generating host either derails it or does no better than a random box of the same size.
   - Donor boxes carved by contribution mass are general machinery, not topic knowledge.
5. **Measurement discipline mattered more than once.**
   - The GSM8K completion mode was void.
   - Short outputs made AST look slow.
   - 503s were counted as wrong answers.
   - A self-distillation control exposed a broken training loss.
   - bf16 gradient noise grows with depth.

   Every one of these would have produced a false conclusion.

## 4. Answers to your questions

**Has cross-model neural map transfer been made effective?**
No, not yet.
- The mapping itself works: stitch KL 0.03–0.6 nats against 4–7 for shuffled pairs, across three donors.
- Injection into a generating host has not beaten its controls:
  - full-strength injection derails every arm;
  - gentle span injection gives the real box 8/12 against 7/12 for a random box.
- Two live tests remain on CWE: a topic-specific (contrastive) donor box, and fixed distillation.

**Have we materially improved the host through transfer from other models?**
No. No measured gain from another model yet. The host improvements so far come from its own boxes and from serving speed.

**Is the performance work baked in everywhere?**
Not yet.
- Fused tiles and AST-with-tiles are on the unmerged branch `claude/amoeba-27b-spec-tiles`. The tile route is opt-in and unqualified, and :8282 serves FN, not tiles.
- Merging is safe: the default layout stays unfused, and nothing changes for existing routes. Making fused the default needs your approval after a settled run.
- The new graded tiers will need re-exporting as fused tiles before they're served.
